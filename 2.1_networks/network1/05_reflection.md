## 🧠 5. Reflection Questions

### 5.1 What would happen if two PCs had the same IP address?
It will be a conflict of IP addresses that will not allow you to connect.

### 5.2 Why do we use a switch instead of directly connecting PCs together?
We use a switch to connect many host devices and PCs. Directly, we can connect only 2 devices.

### 5.3 What does the subnet mask 255.255.255.0 indicate?
It indicates that all portions are network and have the same 8 bits in binary, but the last number can be changed.
